## **STRATEGY 1: SAMPLE ORDERING (BEST - Recommended First)**
### *"Order real samples, photograph them yourself"*

**Timeline:** 2-3 weeks  
**Cost:** $50-200 (depends on quantities)  
**Quality:** Authentic, high-conversion ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

### How It Works:

1. **Find a print-on-demand supplier** that offers paint sticks, art prints, etc.
   - **Printful** (printful.com) - API integration available
   - **Merch by Amazon** (amazon.com/merch) - Free mockups
   - **CustomInk** (customink.com) - Team/artist discounts
   - **Local printers** - Best quality control
   - **Etsy partners** - Easy to work with

2. **Order 1-2 physical samples**
   - Order 1 of each size paint stick with your design
   - Order 1 poster, 1 canvas, 1 wood print, 1 metal print with your artwork
   - Get different finishes (matte, gloss, satin)
   - Include frame options if you offer them

3. **Photograph your samples**
   - **Lighting:** Use natural window light (best) or white LED lights (consistent)
   - **Background:** White poster board or white sheet
   - **Camera:** Phone camera is fine (iPhone 13+ or modern Android)
   - **Multiple angles:**
     - Straight-on (full view)
     - Slight 45-degree angle
     - Close-up of texture/detail
     - Detail of corners/edges
     - Stack of different sizes/colors together

4. **Stage your photos realistically**
   - Paint sticks: In a hand, in a cup, on a desk, arranged artistically
   - Prints: Hung on a wall, propped on a shelf, against a textured wall
   - Canvas: Grouped with home décor items
   - Metal: Showing reflective quality with proper lighting

5. **Edit your photos (optional but recommended)**
   - **Free tools:** Canva, Photoshop Express, Adobe Lightroom Mobile
   - **Adjustments:** Brightness, contrast, saturation, crop
   - **Don't:** Over-filter or make colors unrealistic
   - **Do:** Enhance what's actually there (better lighting, cleaner background)

### Example Photo Set for Paint Sticks:

```
Photo 1: Paint sticks in hand (shows size scale)
Photo 2: Arranged on white surface (shows color options)
Photo 3: Close-up of printed design (shows quality)
Photo 4: In artist's workspace (aspirational, shows use case)
Photo 5: Packaged (shows shipping/unboxing experience)
```

### Cost Breakdown:
- 5 paint stick samples @ $3-5 each = $15-25
- 4 art print samples (poster, canvas, wood, metal) @ $15-40 each = $60-160
- **Total: $75-185** (one-time investment)

## **STRATEGY 2: SUPPLIER MOCKUPS (FAST - Easiest)**
### *"Use mockup generator from your print partner"*

**Timeline:** Same day  
**Cost:** Free to $50 (depends on platform)  
**Quality:** Professional but less authentic ⭐⭐⭐⭐

### How It Works:

1. **Use print supplier's built-in mockup generator**
   - Most POD platforms have mockup generators in their dashboard
   - Upload your design → see it on different products/materials → download PNG

2. **For paint sticks specifically:**
   - Fewer suppliers offer mockups for paint sticks
   - Check: Printful, CustomInk, 4imprint
   - If not available, use Strategy 1 (order samples)

3. **For art prints on different materials:**
   - **Printful mockup generator** - Canvas, posters, metal, wood
   - **Photoshop/Figma templates** - More control, DIY
   - **Canva** - Easy mockup templates

4. **Best free/cheap mockup tools:**
   - **Smartmockups** (smartmockups.com) - Free simple mockups
   - **Placeit** (placeit.net) - $9-25/month, professional
   - **Adobe Mockup Generator** (if you have Adobe subscription)
   - **Figma free templates** - Highly customizable

### When to Use This Strategy:
- ✅ You're just starting and want quick results
- ✅ You need mockups for marketing before ordering samples
- ✅ You want to test different designs fast
- ❌ Don't rely solely on this - eventually order real samples

## 🎨 PAINT STICK SPECIFIC STRATEGY

Paint sticks are unique - here's what works:

### **What to Photograph:**

1. **Single paint stick close-up**
   - Shows design quality and detail
   - White background
   - 45-degree angle to show depth

2. **Multiple paint sticks together**
   - Show different designs/colors side-by-side
   - Fanned out or arranged artistically
   - Shows variety

3. **In-hand shots**
   - Holds paint stick, shows scale relative to hand
   - Casual, relatable
   - Great for Instagram Reels

4. **On a workspace**
   - Paint stick in artist's hand while painting
   - On a palette with paints
   - In a cup/holder with other supplies
   - Shows the actual use case

5. **Packaged/Unboxing**
   - Shows packaging quality
   - Unboxing shot (if you have nice packaging)
   - Ready to ship/gift

### **Lighting Tips for Paint Sticks:**
- Use **diffused natural light** (not direct sun - causes glare)
- **Avoid harsh shadows** - use white reflector opposite light source
- **For metallic/holographic designs** - softer light looks better than bright
- Test with phone camera first - no need for expensive equipment

### **Background Ideas:**
- White poster board (cleanest, best for e-commerce)
- Wood surface (warm, rustic feel)
- Art studio setting (aspirational, authentic)
- Textured wall (modern, designer feel)

## 🔧 PHOTO EDITING QUICK GUIDE

### **Free Online Tools:**

1. **Canva** (canva.com)
   - Adjust brightness, contrast, saturation
   - Add simple text/graphics
   - Straighten/crop
   - Free version is great

2. **Photoshop Express** (photoshop.com/tools)
   - More professional than Canva
   - Better color correction
   - Free

3. **Lightroom Mobile** (adobe.com/lightroom/mobile)
   - Best for color & tone control
   - Free tier sufficient
   - Consistent editing across multiple photos

### **What to Adjust:**

- **Brightness:** Make white background truly white (not gray)
- **Contrast:** Make product stand out from background
- **Saturation:** Enhance your design colors slightly (but keep realistic)
- **Crop:** Remove distracting background elements

### **What NOT to Do:**
- ❌ Don't over-filter or add borders/effects
- ❌ Don't make colors unrealistic (people will notice in person)
- ❌ Don't blur or hide flaws - show reality
- ❌ Don't use too many different lighting styles (inconsistent)

## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Batch your photo shoots**
   - Take all paint stick photos in one session
   - Take all art print photos in another session
   - Consistent lighting throughout = better results

2. **Phone camera settings**
   - Use portrait mode for background blur (if available)
   - Take 3-5 shots of each angle (pick the sharpest)
   - Clean your lens before shooting

3. **Lighting hack**
   - Shoot near a window (best light source)
   - Use white poster board on opposite side to bounce light (DIY reflector)
   - Overcast days = perfect, soft light (better than sunny days)

4. **Stay consistent**
   - Same background for all product shots (looks professional)
   - Similar angles across all products (easier for customers to compare)
   - Edit with same settings (colors look cohesive)

5. **Repurpose photos**
   - Use on product pages (main gallery)
   - Use in Instagram posts (carousel posts showing different angles)
   - Use in TikTok/Reels (zoom in on details, show styling)
   - Use in email newsletters (showcase new products)

## 🚫 COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Fix |
|-----------|--------|
| Only 1 photo per product | Take 5-8 angles minimum |
| Harsh shadows on product | Use diffused light, white reflector |
| Colors look washed out | Boost saturation slightly, check white balance |
| Product blends into background | Use pure white background or contrast surface |
| Inconsistent lighting across photos | Shoot all products in same location, same day |
| Photos too zoomed in | Show whole product + context |
| No lifestyle shots | Include at least 1 styled/in-use shot per product |
| Blurry/low resolution photos | Use phone's highest resolution, keep camera steady |

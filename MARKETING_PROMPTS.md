## 📦 PRODUCT DESCRIPTIONS

### **Prompt 1: Paint Stick Product Description**
```
You are a copywriter for an art supply company. Write a compelling product description for paint sticks with custom artwork.

Details:
- Product: Premium wooden paint sticks with custom artwork printed on them
- Art style: [DESCRIBE YOUR ART STYLE - e.g., "abstract geometric patterns", "landscape watercolors", etc.]
- Target buyer: [DESCRIBE - e.g., "art teachers, craft makers, gift-givers"]
- What makes it special: [YOUR UNIQUE ANGLE - e.g., "hand-designed by artist", "eco-friendly wood", etc.]

Write a 3-4 sentence product description that:
1. Opens with emotion (what feeling does the art evoke?)
2. Explains the practical benefit (what can you do with these?)
3. Mentions uniqueness (why choose these over generic?)
4. Ends with an action word (inspire them to buy)

Keep it under 150 words. Use active voice. Make it feel premium but approachable.
```

## 🎓 USAGE TIPS

### **How to Use These Prompts:**

1. **Copy the prompt exactly** into Claude, ChatGPT, or your favorite AI
2. **Fill in the [BRACKETS]** with your specific details
3. **Adjust tone as needed** - some can be more casual or formal
4. **Use the output as-is** OR edit to make it more "you"
5. **Test on real customers** - see what drives purchases
6. **Keep what works, discard what doesn't** - marketing is iterative

### **Pro Tips:**

- **Customization is key** - The more details you provide, the better the output
- **Authenticity wins** - Let your personality shine through. Don't sound like a robot.
- **Test variations** - Try 2-3 versions of social posts, emails, etc. See which gets better response.
- **Keep it up-to-date** - Update descriptions, bios, and campaign copy as your work evolves
- **Collect feedback** - Ask customers what made them buy. Use their language in future copy.
